◆Enterprise Solutions Portfolio

Four enterprise platforms, each designed to turn a complex operating reality into a governed digital environment.

01

NX-LAM

Logistics Asset & Operations Platform

An enterprise operating platform designed to help logistics organizations run their asset base and field operations from a single digital environment, replacing the spreadsheets and disconnected tools that usually sit between operations, finance and management.

It was built around the way logistics businesses actually operate: assets are acquired and assigned, contracts and leases carry obligations, projects consume resources, and executives need a reliable view of all of it while the work is still in motion.

Core capabilities

  • Operational asset and fleet management across the full ownership lifecycle
  • Contract and leasing management with the obligations attached to each agreement
  • Project setup, resource assignment and progress monitoring
  • Executive performance reporting drawn from live operational data
  • A unified operating environment connecting execution, financial controls and governance

Where it fits

Logistics and transportation organizations managing owned or leased assets across multiple projects, sites or contracting parties.

02

NX-iWork

AI Business Operations Platform

An AI-native enterprise operating platform that lets an organization deploy specialized AI agents into its actual business workflows — not as a chat assistant bolted onto the side, but as participants in execution, coordination and decision support.

The governing idea is that enterprise AI only becomes useful when it operates inside defined boundaries: an agent should know its scope, act within approved workflows, and leave a trace management can review. The platform is built around that principle.

Core capabilities

  • Deployment of specialized AI agents mapped to business functions
  • Workflow orchestration across multi-step, multi-party processes
  • Intelligent coordination between teams, systems and agents
  • Decision support built on the organization’s own operational context
  • Governed execution, so automated work stays auditable and within policy

Where it fits

Organizations that want AI embedded in day-to-day operations with governance and accountability intact, rather than isolated experiments.

03

IBP

Insurance Brokerage Platform

An integrated operating platform for Saudi insurance brokerage companies, covering the brokerage business end to end: the customer relationship, the policy workflow, the money, the insurer connectivity and the regulatory obligations that surround all of it.

Brokerages typically run these functions across separate systems, which makes reconciliation slow and compliance manual. IBP brings them into one operating environment designed around local regulatory reality.

Core capabilities

  • Brokerage operations across the policy lifecycle
  • Customer management and servicing workflows
  • Financial controls over receivables, settlements and commissions
  • Insurer connectivity for quotes, issuance and policy data
  • Regulatory workflows with ZATCA-compliant financial operations

Where it fits

Licensed insurance brokerages in Saudi Arabia that need operational efficiency and regulatory compliance in the same system.

04

Naqlah

Digital Freight Exchange Platform

A digital freight exchange that connects licensed logistics providers with businesses that need to move goods, replacing broker phone calls and informal arrangements with a structured, transactional marketplace.

The design question was how to make a two-sided freight market trustworthy: only licensed carriers participate, requirements are expressed in a structured form, and assignment is driven by fit rather than by whoever answers first.

Core capabilities

  • A marketplace connecting licensed logistics providers with shippers
  • Structured operational workflows from request through delivery
  • Commercial transactions handled inside the platform
  • Intelligent assignment matching loads to suitable providers

Where it fits

Businesses with recurring transportation needs, and licensed logistics providers looking for qualified demand.
